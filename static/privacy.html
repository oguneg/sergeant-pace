What happens to your details

Sergeant Pace is a hobby project, not a company product. This page says in plain words what it stores, who else sees it, and how to remove it.

  • You enter body numbers and running details. They are kept for {{state_days}} days after you last use the app, and you can download or delete them at any time.
  • What you type or say, and your numbers, are sent to Google's Gemini AI to write the sergeant's replies. {{google_short}}
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.
  • This is a coaching demo, not medical advice.

Health information and your consent

Weight, body fat, age and notes about pain or injury are health-related. Data protection law (GDPR) treats that as a special category, so I only use it with your explicit consent. You give that consent by ticking the box before you send the intake form. Nothing is sent from your browser before then. You can withdraw consent at any time by pressing Reset recruit, which deletes your file, and by not using the app.

Your rights

  • See and download your data: open Training file in the app and choose Download my data, or open this link in the same browser.
  • Delete it: open Training file and choose Reset recruit.
  • Correct, restrict or object: report new numbers through the app, or email me.
  • Complain: you can complain to the Swedish data protection authority, IMY.

There are no accounts, so I cannot tell whose file is whose. That means I can only act on the data in your own browser session, which is why the buttons above do the work.

Who it is for

Adults. It is not meant for children under 16.

Not medical advice

The limits in this app are a coaching demo and have not been checked by a doctor or physiotherapist. If you feel pain, dizziness or chest tightness, stop and see a doctor. Do not rely on this app for medical decisions.
